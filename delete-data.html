Personal Data Deletion Request — BlueOra

Last updated: November 7, 2026.

Under GDPR and Google Play user data policies, any BlueOra user can request the deletion of their personal data or account at any time.

Option 1: Directly inside the BlueOra mobile app

  1. Open BlueOra.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings gear icon.
  3. Scroll to Account & Security → tap "Delete my account".

Option 2: By email request to support

Send an email to: contact@blue-ora.com

Subject: BlueOra Data Deletion Request

Please specify your account email address. Requests are processed within 30 days maximum.

What data is deleted?

User credentials, profile, synchronized dive logs, statistics, community posts and centre reviews are completely erased.
