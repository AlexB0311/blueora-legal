Account & Personal Data Deletion Request — BlueOra

Last updated: November 7, 2026.

In accordance with GDPR, international privacy standards, and Google Play developer requirements, users of the BlueOra scuba diving application (published by Creao Tech) have the full right to request the permanent and irreversible deletion of their account and all associated personal data at any time.

Option 1: Directly inside the BlueOra mobile app

If you have the BlueOra application installed on your device:

  1. Open the BlueOra application.
  2. Navigate to the Profile tab (bottom right icon).
  3. Tap the Settings gear icon (top right).
  4. Scroll down to the Account & Security section.
  5. Tap "Delete my account" and confirm your choice.

Immediate effect: Your account is immediately deactivated, session tokens are revoked, and all synchronized personal records are queued for permanent deletion.

Option 2: Without the app (Online request by email)

If you have uninstalled the app or no longer have access to your mobile device, you can submit a deletion request via email:

  • Send an email to: contact@blue-ora.com
  • Subject: BlueOra Account and Data Deletion Request
  • Body: Please specify the email address associated with your BlueOra account and your username.

Send Account Deletion Request

Processing timeline: Your request will be fulfilled within a maximum of 30 calendar days.

What data is deleted?

Account deletion permanently and irreversibly erases:

  • Your account credentials (email address, encrypted password, authentication tokens).
  • Your public diver profile (username, bio, avatar, certification level, experience statistics).
  • Your entire synchronized dive logbook (dive depth profiles, max depth, duration, notes, marine fauna logs).
  • Your posts, photos, and comments in the community feed.
  • Your reviews and ratings left on dive centre listings.

Data retention policy

No personal data is retained after deletion. Only an anonymized, timestamped technical audit log (confirming that deletion occurred for compliance) is kept for 12 months for security and legal requirements, completely decoupled from any diving records or personal identity.
