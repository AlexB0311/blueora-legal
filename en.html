BlueOra Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026.

BlueOra is a dive logbook and destination guide published by Creao Studio, the data controller. Contact: contact@blue-ora.com.

Data we process

  • Account: email address, username, display name, profile photo, level and certifications you add.
  • Dives: date, location, depth, duration, dive profile, notes, photos and files you import (FIT, UDDF, CSV, JSON) or read over Bluetooth from your dive computer.
  • Location: only when you ask for it (to geotag a dive, find clubs or dive sites). It is never published without your consent.
  • Community content: posts, comments, dive centre reviews, groups and reports.
  • Notifications: push notifications are disabled in version 1.3.1; this version does not register new push tokens.

BlueOra shows no ads, uses no advertising trackers and never sells data.

Why

To provide the service (legal basis: performance of a contract): keeping your logbook, syncing it across your devices, showing the community, dive centres and destinations, and ensuring moderation and security (legitimate interest).

Service providers

  • Supabase: database, authentication and file hosting.
  • Expo / Firebase Cloud Messaging: providers planned for future notifications; no push delivery in version 1.3.1.
  • Open-Meteo, OpenStreetMap: marine weather and maps; destination coordinates for weather; nearby searches also use your requested position as described below are sent to them.
  • Booking.com: hotel links open its website; no account data is shared with it.

All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Where your data is stored: the database, accounts and files are hosted by Supabase in the European Union (Ireland). Some technical providers (Expo, Google Firebase, Supabase support) may access data from the United States; these transfers rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Dive safety: BlueOra is a logbook and a guide. It does not replace your dive computer, your training or your dive centre's instructions; do not use it to plan a dive or decide on its safety. Information about dive centres, hotels, prices and weather is indicative.

Retention

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. Offline data stays on your phone until you uninstall the app.

Delete your account and data

In the app: Profile → Settings → “Delete my account”. Deletion is immediate and permanent: account, profile, synced dives, posts and files.

Without the app: email contact@blue-ora.com from your account address with the subject “BlueOra account deletion”. We delete the account and all associated data within 30 days at most. A technical log of the deletion (identifier and date) is kept for one year for security reasons.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export your data, object to its processing or request its erasure by writing to contact@blue-ora.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL, cnil.fr) or your local authority.

Minors

BlueOra is intended for people aged 16 and over.

Additional data disclosures — 1 November 2026

Your optional date of birth is used for the birthday discount. Community uploads can include photos and videos. We store the version and date of your accepted community terms and the users you block to operate safety features.

Open-Meteo receives destination coordinates. When you request nearby spots, Overpass (overpass-api.de) receives your current position rounded to three decimals, roughly one hundred metres; this is still precise location for Google Play purposes. Map tile servers receive the area viewed and the network information needed to serve requests. Data is not sold or used for advertising.

Offline copies belong to each account and can be cleared in Settings; they are removed on sign-out or uninstall. Blocking and community acceptance records are deleted with the account. See the community terms.
