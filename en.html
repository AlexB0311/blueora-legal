BlueOra Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026.

BlueOra is a dive logbook and destination guide published by Creao Studio, the data controller. Contact: contact@blue-ora.com.

Data we process

  • Account: email address, username, display name, profile photo, level and certifications you add.
  • Dives: date, location, depth, duration, dive profile, notes, photos and files you import (FIT, UDDF, CSV, JSON) or read over Bluetooth from your dive computer.
  • Location: only when you ask for it (to geotag a dive, find clubs or dive sites). It is never published without your consent.
  • Community content: posts, comments, dive centre reviews, groups and reports.
  • Notifications: a device token used to send the notifications you turned on.

BlueOra shows no ads, uses no advertising trackers and never sells data.

Why

To provide the service (legal basis: performance of a contract): keeping your logbook, syncing it across your devices, showing the community, dive centres and destinations, and ensuring moderation and security (legitimate interest).

Service providers

  • Supabase: database, authentication and file hosting.
  • Expo / Firebase Cloud Messaging: notification delivery.
  • Open-Meteo, OpenStreetMap: marine weather and maps; only destination coordinates are sent to them.
  • Booking.com: hotel links open its website; no account data is shared with it.

All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Retention

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. Offline data stays on your phone until you uninstall the app.

Delete your account and data

In the app: Profile → Settings → “Delete my account”. Deletion is immediate and permanent: account, profile, synced dives, posts and files.

Without the app: email contact@blue-ora.com from your account address with the subject “BlueOra account deletion”. We delete the account and all associated data within 30 days at most. A technical log of the deletion (identifier and date) is kept for one year for security reasons.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export your data, object to its processing or request its erasure by writing to contact@blue-ora.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL, cnil.fr) or your local authority.

Minors

BlueOra is intended for people aged 16 and over.
